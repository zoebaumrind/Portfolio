04 · About

Hello! I'm Zoe Baumrind.

I'm interested in how ideas get developed, executed, and experienced: why certain products, platforms, and moments connect with people and others don't. I got into marketing because I wanted to understand that, and into analytics because I wanted to prove it.

I'm a senior at Binghamton University's School of Management studying Marketing and Business Analytics. Along the way I've done strategic communications at a creative agency, procurement and production for Anheuser-Busch brands at HH Global, run a student consulting division, led the campus organization for business, advertising, and media careers, and spent a semester in Barcelona studying marketing research and social media marketing.

Off the clock: dance, fitness, fashion, reality television with the group chat open, and an ongoing project to explain to my friends why the thing they just sent me is about to be everywhere.
