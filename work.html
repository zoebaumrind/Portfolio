StrategyAnalytics

Paid media platform analysis: LinkedIn Ads vs. Google Ads

C&A Digital · Strategic Communications Intern

White Plains, NY · Summer 2025

The problem

A boutique agency needed to decide where paid budgets for B2B healthcare clients should go. Leadership wanted a clear answer, not a vendor pitch.

What I did

  • Evaluated platform performance and engagement metrics across LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads, using audience insights, cost-per-click data, and ROI forecasting.
  • Framed the comparison around the client's real goals: reaching healthcare decision-makers, not just generating clicks.
  • Presented the analysis and a recommendation to agency leadership.

What came of it

The analysis gave leadership a defensible framework for paid media strategy on B2B healthcare campaigns, and became my first experience turning a spreadsheet into a room-level decision.

Deliverable: comparison analysis, recommendation deck, presentation to leadership.
