StrategyAnalytics

Paid media platform analysis: LinkedIn Ads vs. Google Ads

C&A Digital · Strategic Communications Intern

White Plains, NY · Summer 2025

The problem

C&A Digital, the boutique agency I was interning for, needed to decide how to spend its own advertising budget: Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, or what mix of the two.

What I did

  • Evaluated platform performance and engagement metrics across LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads, using audience insights, cost-per-click data, and ROI forecasting.
  • Framed the comparison around the agency's real goal: reaching the healthcare, nonprofit, and B2B decision-makers who hire agencies, not just generating clicks.
  • Presented the analysis and a recommendation to agency leadership.

What came of it

The analysis gave leadership a defensible basis for how to split the agency's advertising budget between the two platforms.

Deliverable: comparison analysis, recommendation deck, presentation to leadership.

LeadershipConsulting

TAMID: from education member to Director of Consulting

TAMID Group at Binghamton

Binghamton, NY · 2024 – 2026

  1. 01Education memberJoined TAMIDLearned the consulting fundamentals, case structure, and the Israeli startup ecosystem before applying to the consulting track.
  2. 02Consulting Track Committee MemberAugust 2024 – December 2025Workshops and simulated engagements on real problems, like improving the campus dining experience. First client: Reloop, where I ran market research and business strategy analysis to support its expansion.
  3. 03Project Manager, FitextJanuary – April 2025Led a consulting team and served as the primary liaison to the client's executive team. Recruited and onboarded new members to cover staffing gaps and built a targeted outreach database of 200+ micro-influencers.
  4. 04Director of ConsultingMay 2025 – June 2026Ran the consulting division: 12+ international startups, 5 project managers, and a seat on the chapter executive board.

The problem

A student consulting program only works if the work is good enough for real clients. Each step up meant more responsibility for making sure it was: first for my own research, then for a team's deliverables, then for every engagement the chapter took on.

What I did

  • As a consulting track member, conducted market research and business strategy analysis for Reloop, delivering actionable recommendations to support its expansion, while sharpening research, data analysis, and public speaking through weekly workshops.
  • As project manager for Fitext, led influencer recruitment strategy, kept all deliverables on schedule, acted as the main point of contact for the client's executives, and solved a staffing shortage by recruiting and onboarding new team members.
  • As Director of Consulting, oversaw all project managers and guided teams of 4 to 6 consultants through Israeli startup engagements, ensuring quality deliverables, ongoing client communication, and alignment with TAMID's mission.
  • Served on the chapter executive board, collaborated with national leadership to match projects and report progress, and mentored project managers on consulting methodology, client relations, and leadership.

What came of it

Consistent, client-aligned deliverables across a dozen engagements, a bench of project managers ready to run their own teams.

Deliverable: Reloop expansion recommendations; Fitext influencer outreach database and engagement strategy; chapter-wide engagement staffing, oversight, and PM mentorship.
